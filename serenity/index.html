Our flagship product

Serenity

Serenity is a voice-first assistant built by XFire SRL for tradespeople, freelancers and small business owners. When a client calls and you can't answer, Serenity picks up the message, transcribes it, summarises it and organises it automatically.

No more missed calls turning into lost clients. Every voicemail becomes a clear, actionable request — sorted by urgency, ready to act on from the app.

Serenity is available on iOS and Android, with a free trial and no setup required.

Discover Serenity → serenityapp.be

What Serenity does

  • Transcribes voicemails and calls into clear text
  • Automatically organises and prioritises client requests
  • Syncs with your calendar to plan appointments
